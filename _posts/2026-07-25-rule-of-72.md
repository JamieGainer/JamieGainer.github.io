I was thinking about quantifying how prices have changed lately, 
and it was making me think of "the rule of 72", 
which is a rough heuristic for how long it takes to double 
your money at $p\%$ interest.  (The answer is $72 / p$).

To understand why this is true we solve for $n$ in the equation
$$
(1 + p/100)^n = 2.
$$

Taking the (natural) log of both sides, we obtain
$$
n (\log{1 + p/100}) = \log{2}.
$$
